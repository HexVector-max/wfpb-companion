Forks Over Knives Living Principles

100% Oil-Free Cooking

Extracted oil is 4,000 cal/lb of pure fat without fiber. Sauté with 1–2 tbsp hot vegetable broth or water in a heated non-stick or stainless steel pan.

Intact Carbs = Satiety

Potatoes, oats, sweet potatoes, and brown rice trigger stomach stretch receptors at 500 cal/lb. Starches keep you from craving ultra-processed junk.

Supplement Vitamin B12

B12 is produced by soil microorganisms, not animals. Take cyanocobalamin (500–1,000 mcg 2–3 times a week) from day one.
